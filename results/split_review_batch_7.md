**Question:**

Application: While Section 358 preserves the procedural framework for investigating IPC-era offences, it does not override substantive defences. This creates a hierarchy where the general exception in Section 20 supersedes the savings clause, meaning that even if the IPC permitted proceedings against such children, the BNS Section 20 defence would apply to bar them.
Conclusion: Proceedings are barred by Section 20 despite the savings clause in Section 358.

Core legal principle: General exceptions apply to preserved offences despite savings clauses maintaining procedural continuity.
